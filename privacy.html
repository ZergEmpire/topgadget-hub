Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

TopGadget Hub (“the app”) is a private desktop tool used by one person — the author of the YouTube channel Kavalerov TopGadget — to publish his own videos and posts to his own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. The app is not offered to other users.

What the app accesses

Only the author’s own accounts, after he signs in himself through the official sign-in of each platform:

  • TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API): basic profile information (open ID, nickname, avatar) to show which account will receive the post; the creator’s posting settings (available privacy levels, interaction settings, maximum video length); publishing the author’s videos and reading their status; the view, like, comment and share counts of the author’s own videos.
  • YouTube: uploading the author’s videos, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and tags, adding them to his playlists, and reading view, like and comment counts of his own videos.
  • Instagram: publishing the author’s photos, carousels and Reels and reading the statistics of his own posts.

Where data is stored

Only on the author’s own computer. Access tokens and publication records are kept in local files with access restricted to the computer owner. They are never sent to anyone except the platform the content is published to.

Instagram downloads post files by link only. For an Instagram post the app makes copies of that post’s files available through a tunnelling service for the few minutes of publishing, under random addresses, and deletes them right after publishing. No other data passes through this service.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect data about other people.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer data to third parties.
  • It does not show ads and does not use the data for any other purpose.

Revoking access and deleting data

The author can revoke the app’s access at any time in the security / app permissions section of the respective platform’s settings (TikTok, Google Account, Instagram — Settings → Apps and websites) and delete the local files of the app.
