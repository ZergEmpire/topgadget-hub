Publishing tool of the Kavalerov TopGadget channel

TopGadget Hub is a private desktop application used by one person — the author of the tech review channel Kavalerov TopGadget — to publish his own finished videos and posts to his own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram from one place.

How it works

  1. The author signs in to his own account on each platform himself (official OAuth sign-in of the platform).
  2. He reviews the video, title, description, hashtags and settings in the app and edits them if needed.
  3. He chooses who can see the post and confirms publishing. Nothing is uploaded before he presses “Publish”.
  4. The app uploads the video through the platform’s official API and shows the publishing status.

TikTok

With the author’s permission the app uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API (Direct Post) to post his videos to his own TikTok account, shows his nickname before posting, lets him choose the privacy level and interaction settings, and reads the view, like, comment and share counts of his own videos.
